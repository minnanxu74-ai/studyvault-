Counting Down by 2

Learn how to count backward by 2s by taking away two each time.

Lesson progress0%
Learn first

🌟 Counting down by 2 means taking away two

When we count down by 2, the numbers get smaller by 2 each time.

10 → 8 → 6 → 4 → 2
Start at 10. Take away 2 each time: 10, 8, 6, 4, 2.

We can also start at 20:

20 → 18 → 16 → 14 → 12 → 10
Each new number is 2 less than the number before it.

Think of it like taking two steps backward at a time.

Remember: counting down by 2 means taking away two each time.
